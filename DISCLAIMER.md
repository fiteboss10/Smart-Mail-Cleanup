## Recommendations

To minimise the risk of unintended data loss:

1. **Always use Dry Run first.** Review the expanded results — including individual message subjects and senders — before clicking Run Now, especially when testing new rules.
2. **Use Move to Trash or Move to Found folder** rather than Permanently Delete until you are fully confident your rules are correct.
3. **Export your rules regularly** using the Export JSON function so you can restore them if needed.
4. **Start with a small folder** (e.g. Inbox only) before expanding to your full mailbox.
